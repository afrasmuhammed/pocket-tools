How to add stickers to WhatsApp

  1. Download the ZIP file and unzip it to get the .webp files.
  2. Install Sticker Maker for WhatsApp on your phone — App Store or Play Store.
  3. Open the app, create a new sticker pack, import the .webp files, and tap Add to WhatsApp.

For best results, use images with a clear subject or transparent background (PNG).
